Logo / icon exploration

The current apps/web/public/favicon.svg is a generic slanted abstract mark — 15 layered, blurred gradient ellipses under a mask, which is expensive to render and won't hold up at 16px, and has no connection to the name "RootEd." These 5 concepts explore real, name-driven alternatives, each tested at the sizes that actually matter: a browser tab (16px), an app icon tile, and a nav-bar lockup next to the wordmark — plus a true single-color knockout, not just a desaturation filter.

Recommendation: Concept 1, Root Network. "RootEd" reads two ways at once here — a literal root system, and a branching data/org-chart network (school → classes → students; tenant → roles → users) — which is exactly what the product does. It also survives 16px better than any other concept here: 4 strokes and 4 dots, nothing finer. Concept 5 (Evolved current) is the safe fallback if you'd rather not spend any brand equity right now — it keeps the exact silhouette already shipped everywhere, just fixes the render-cost and small-size problems.

03Monogram Root-R

Optimises: an ownable, brandable letterform — a bold geometric "R" whose leg splits into two root tendrils. Works as a standalone monogram the way "M" does for Mongoose or "N" does for Notion. "App icon" shows both a white tile (icon in full brand gradient) and a gradient tile (icon knocked out to solid white), matching how most apps ship two variants.
