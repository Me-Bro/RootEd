Security

Your staff's bank details and your students' records — protected on purpose, not by accident.

School data is sensitive: staff government IDs, bank accounts, salaries, student records. Here's specifically what protects it.

01

Tenant isolation enforced at the database layer

Every query is automatically checked for your school's tenant ID before it runs. A bug that tries to read another school's data fails outright — it doesn't quietly return the wrong rows.

02

Encrypted staff PII

Government ID, bank account and salary fields are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with a separate encryption key derived per school — not one shared key for every tenant.

03

Immutable audit log

Every change to sensitive records is written to an audit log as a before/after diff that can't be edited after the fact — you can always answer "who changed this, and when."
