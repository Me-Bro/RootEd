9 · Report Cards

A long-running background job triggered from a device that locks its screen after 30 seconds. The design problem is not the button — it is trust: knowing the PDFs got built, and getting them to parents.

Pain today: two selects and a Generate button, then in-page polling every 3 s for up to 2 minutes (MAX_POLL_ATTEMPTS = 40). Lock the phone and the poll dies with no notification; the history table is the only way back. The job is queued per section+term, so end-of-term means repeating the same three taps 40 times. Nothing checks whether grades are actually locked first.
· real data: 6 seeded batches, 20 of 40 sections locked for Term 1
VARIANT 1
Job card + push SHIP

Optimises: trust on a phone that sleeps. Progress ring with real counts, an explicit "you can leave this screen" promise, and a notification when it lands.
