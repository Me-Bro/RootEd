1000 students, 40 sections. Two completely different jobs share this screen: find one child fast (front desk, parent on the phone) and work through a cohort (admin, class teacher).

Pain today: a 4-column table plus Previous/Next paging at 20 rows a page — 50 pages for this dataset. The section filter is a <select> with 40 optgrouped options (the same control is repeated in 5 places, and it never remembers your last choice). The Status column clips at 390 px, and Add Student opens a 6-field modal that a phone keyboard covers.
· real data: 1000 students, Grade 1–10 × A–D
