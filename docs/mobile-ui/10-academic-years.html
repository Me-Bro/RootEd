10 · Academic Years & Terms

Touched twice a year, but it sets the context every other screen filters by. Right now half of it has no interface at all — and that omission is what makes the term dropdowns ambiguous everywhere else.

Pain today: the page lists years in a five-column table with an Activate button — and terms have no UI whatsoever, even though Grades, Grade Report and Report Cards all filter by term (creating one is an API-only operation). Because those screens call /academic/terms without ?yearId, three seeded years render as "Term 1, Term 2, Term 1, Term 2, Term 1, Term 2".
