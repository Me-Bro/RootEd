5 · Enter Marks

Twenty-five numbers, typed at 10 pm on a sofa, from a paper mark sheet. Accuracy matters more than speed — but speed is what makes teachers do it in the app instead of on paper.

Pain today: four selects (section / term / subject / assessment) gate the table, and the term list is fetched without ?yearId — with three academic years seeded it renders "Term 1, Term 2, Term 1, Term 2, Term 1, Term 2". Then 25 <input type=number> cells: the OS keyboard covers ~45% of the screen, every field needs tap → type → dismiss, and the locked banner scrolls off the top so people type into disabled fields. One Save at the bottom.
· real data: Grade 5-A Mathematics, Term 1 final, class avg 69
