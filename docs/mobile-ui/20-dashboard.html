The first screen a principal sees after login, every day. Worth comparing more than 2 approaches before locking one in — a bad landing screen costs a principal something every single morning, not just once.

Pain today: every non-super-admin tenant member — principal, teacher, accountant, librarian — lands on the identical 4 cards: school name, plan, status, timezone. Zero attendance, fee, grade or staff data anywhere. No role- or permission-aware branching exists beyond systemRole === 'super_admin'.
· real data: Test School, Fri 21 Aug 2026 · 962 students, 74 staff, 40 sections
VARIANT 1
Plain KPI grid

Optimises: zero new interaction to learn — the exact same static 2×2 grid the desktop already uses, just filled with real numbers instead of metadata. Trades away: no drill-down, no prioritisation; a principal still opens 4 separate modules to act on anything they see here.

Recommendation: ship V2 as the landing layout — it's the only concept that both surfaces today's exceptions and keeps every KPI's exact value on screen, which is what a "first impression" screen needs to earn every morning, not just once. Borrow V3's ring treatment for the 4 KPI cards themselves — swapping a flat percentage for a small ring costs nothing and reads faster. V4 and V5 are strong ideas for a future "Insights" or "Digest" tab, but neither can be the only Home view: V4 hides 3 of 4 domains until you swipe, and V5 hides steady-state problems that had no new activity today.
