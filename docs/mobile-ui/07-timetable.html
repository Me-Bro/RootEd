7 · Timetable Builder

The worst screen on a phone today, and the one with the hardest constraints: three unique indexes (section, teacher, room per day+period) mean a wrong tap is a server error, not a warning.

Pain today: an 8 × 5 <table> (40 cells) inside overflow-x-auto — at 390 px you see about 1.6 days, so editing means horizontal scroll-hunting. Each cell opens a 5-field modal (teacher, subject, start, end, room), and conflicts only surface as a 409 after submit, discarding what you typed. Publish/unpublish and "copy from another year" hide in the desktop header.
· real data: Grade 5-A, 1600 seeded slots, 30 of 40 sections published
